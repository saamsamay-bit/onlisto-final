📧 Email setup guide — auto-import disputes from Gmail (2 minutes per client)

For NEW clients (easiest): register their OnBuy account with an @onlisto.io email from day one — every dispute email lands in your system automatically.

For EXISTING clients: set up auto-forward from their Gmail:

  1. In the client's Gmail, open Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address.
  2. Enter their dispute inbox: disputes+clientname@onlisto.io and confirm the verification email (it arrives in your Onlisto inbox).
  3. Go to Settings → Filters → Create a new filter: in From put customersupport-gb@onbuy.com, in Has the words put dispute OR chargeback OR escalation OR "we need your help".
  4. Click Create filter → tick Forward it to: and choose disputes+clientname@onlisto.io → Create filter.

The Cloud Function parser reads every forwarded email automatically: order ID, dispute reference, reason, deadline, fee amount and type are extracted and appear in the table above. When a real email arrives, the parser patterns are refined in one patch.
